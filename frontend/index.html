Legal & Compliance · India DPDP Act 2023

        
Terms, Privacy & Refund Policy

      

      
    

    

      

        1. Terms & Conditions

        Sentinel-DPDP provides automated static analysis, remediation previews, and pre-merge verification gates. Customers retain 100% ownership of their source code. Sentinel-DPDP is a technical compliance engineering tool and does not constitute formal legal representation.
      

      

        2. DPDP Act 2023 Privacy Policy (Zero Source Retention)

        Public/private repositories cloned during Cloud SaaS scans are analyzed in ephemeral temporary containers and purged immediately after verification. Only anonymized metadata (risk score, finding counts, DPDP sections) is written to BigQuery. Enterprise VPC customers process 100% of code and PII inside their own infrastructure. Under DPDP Section 12, you may request data erasure at any time via privacy@sentinel-dpdp.in.
      

      

        3. Razorpay UPI Autopay & Cancellation Policy

        Subscriptions renew monthly via RBI-compliant UPI Autopay or e-Mandate cards. You may pause or cancel your mandate in 1 click before the next billing cycle with zero cancellation fee. Duplicate charges are refunded within 5–7 business days.
